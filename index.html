01 — Our story

From needles
to portafilters.

Höfn started the way all good Dundee ideas start — two local tattoo artists, too much espresso, and a stubborn belief that this city deserved a better coffee shop.

So we built one. Minimalist walls, funky artwork on rotation, and a bar pouring some of the sharpest coffee in Scotland — roasted for us by Unorthodox Roasters.

No fuss. No syrups with silly names. Just proper coffee, Italian pastry, and a room that looks as good as the flat white tastes.
